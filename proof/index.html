How to read it

Seven stops, about a minute.

Light
Checked here, in your browser. Anything only outlined is someone else’s word.
The key
The gold star: the one key this page trusts, built into it. You can trust the other publisher’s key instead, in the list on the left.
The gate
The list of validators that key signed: one light for each of the 35. The gate is only drawn once your browser has checked the list’s signature.
A light
Lit when your browser has checked that validator’s signature on this ledger. The lines beyond it are its spectrum, set by the bytes it signed, so they change with every ledger.
The horizon
It opens once 28 of them have signed the same ledger. That is the quorum.
The heart
Its light comes on when the ledger’s header hashes to exactly what they signed.
The rings
Behind the gate, one for each ledger proven here while you watched, oldest furthest back.
An account
Look one up and it appears beyond the gate as an outline, the relay’s word. When its path through the state tree hashes all the way up to the state root in the heart, the path lights and the body with it.
A receipt
Paste a transaction hash in the box. Your browser proves it and makes a PDF receipt that carries its own proof: drop it back on this page, now or years from now, and every check runs again.
